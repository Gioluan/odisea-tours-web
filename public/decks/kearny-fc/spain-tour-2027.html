Chapter V · Reservations

Securing
your place.

A €200 deposit per person reserves a spot, on a first-come, first-served basis. The June 15th deposit is what allows us to begin securing accommodation, training grounds, match fixtures and stadium slots in your name.

Why June 15th matters
Hotels, training facilities at the RFEF, Valencia CF and FC Barcelona, and La Liga match tickets are blocked nine months out. Earlier deposits secure better rooms, prime training slots and the right fixture windows.
Squad & family rooms
Once your deposits are in, we begin allocating rooms by squad and by family unit. The earlier we know who is traveling, the better we can match.
Cancellation
Deposits are non-refundable but transferable to another traveler in your squad up to 60 days before departure.
