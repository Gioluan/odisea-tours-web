Chapter IV · The Package

What’s
included.

Pricing covers everything below for the full 10-day programme, based on a confirmed group of 100 PAX traveling together.

Accommodation
9 nights total · 4★ hotel in Castelldefels (3 nights) and tournament accommodation in San Sebastián (6 nights). Rooms allocated by squad and family.
Meals
Breakfast & dinner daily at hotels in Barcelona. Full board at the Velódromo de Anoeta during the Donosti Cup. Lunches in transit at own cost.
Ground Transport
Private coach transfers throughout: airport pickups, Barcelona ↔ San Sebastián, training, match-day & tournament shuttles.
Soccer Programme
FC Barcelona training session at Joan Gamper, preparation match against local opposition, and full Donosti Cup tournament participation across all six tournament days.
Tournament Entry
Donosti Cup registration fees, credentials, age verification, fixture allocation, opening & closing ceremonies and Authentics Day fan zone.
Tour Management
Odisea Tours guide on the ground for the full 10 days, certificates ceremony, group photography, and daily logistics.
Cultural & Family Experience
Free time built into every day. La Concha bay, Monte Igueldo, pintxos in San Sebastián’s Old Town, Sagrada Família and Las Ramblas.
Not Included
International flights into and out of Barcelona, all lunches in transit, personal expenses & gratuities.

San Sebastián hotels fill up months in advance for the Donosti Cup window. An early August deposit is what secures the best accommodation, prime tournament categories and the FC Barcelona training slot.
