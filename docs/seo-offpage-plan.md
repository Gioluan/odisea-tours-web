# Odisea Tours: Off-Page Authority Plan

Last updated 2026-06-08.

The on-page and technical SEO for odisea-tours.com is already strong: pillar pages, geo hubs (USA, UK, Australia + city and 15 state guides), tour pages, journal pillar posts, full schema (TravelAgency, WebSite, FAQ, Place, Person, TouristTrip, HowTo, BreadcrumbList), hreflang, sitemap, robots and llms.txt.

What is NOT yet built is off-page authority. For competitive head terms like "soccer tours spain" the page-one incumbents (IFX, Premier International, Eture, European Soccer Solutions, Spanish Soccer Trials, DBS) win mostly on domain authority and backlinks, not on-page craft. This is the work that moves the ceiling.

## 1. Backlinks from real soccer clubs and partners (highest ROI)

A single contextual link from a youth soccer club that toured with Odisea is worth more than fifty directory links. They are topically perfect and hard for competitors to replicate.

- **[JUAN]** Ask past and current clients to add a line on their site: "Our Spain tour was organised by Odisea Tours" linking to the relevant page. Best candidates: Hawaii Elite, Long Beach University, NorCal coaching contacts, any club with a trip recap or gallery page. A club "Trips" or "News" page is the natural home.
- **[JUAN]** Tony Dress partnership: a link from Tony's site or newsletter to the relevant tour page.
- **[BOTH]** I draft a short, specific link-request email per relationship (not a mass template). Cold first-touch rules apply: gatekeeper-sized, single ask, no proposals attached.
- **[CODE]** Add a discreet "As featured by" / "Clubs we have hosted" logo strip on the site once any partner agrees to be named, which gives them a reciprocal reason to link back. Only with prior client permission, no fabricated logos.

- **[BOTH]** I draft the review-request message and a simple follow-up. You decide who receives it.
- **[CODE]** Once real reviews exist, add `Review` / `AggregateRating` schema (only with genuine reviews, never invented) and a testimonials section sourced from real, named, permissioned clients.

## 5. Citations and directory listings (consistency layer)

Lower ROI than club links, but they reinforce the entity and NAP. Quality over volume, no spam directories.

- **[BOTH]** Submit to relevant, real industry directories: sports-travel and educational-travel directories, Spanish DMC / tourism listings, and any association Odisea genuinely belongs to (do not claim memberships Odisea does not hold).
- **[JUAN]** If Odisea is a member of any travel trade body (ASTA, USTOA, a Spanish tourism association), get the member-directory listing with a backlink. If not a member, this is a separate decision, not an SEO task.

The Ground Agents ecosystem can pass a little relevant authority, but over-linking a small owned network looks manufactured. A few contextual links only.
